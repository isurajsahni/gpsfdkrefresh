# Canvas Size Guide: How to Choose the Right Wall Art Size

The most common wall art mistake is not the wrong style. It is the wrong size. A canvas that is too small looks lost on the wall; one that is too big overwhelms the room. This canvas size guide gives you simple rules, a size chart for every room, and layout ideas so you get it right the first time.

## Rule 1: The Two-Thirds Rule

When art hangs above furniture, it should be about **two-thirds (60 to 75 percent) of the furniture's width**.

- 3-seater sofa, 180 cm (72 in) wide: about 110 to 135 cm (44 to 54 in) of art
- Queen bed, 150 cm (60 in) wide: about 90 to 110 cm (36 to 44 in) of art
- TV unit or console, 150 cm (60 in) wide: about 90 to 110 cm (36 to 44 in) of art

If you are hanging two or three canvases together, the two-thirds rule applies to the whole group, including the gaps.

## Rule 2: The Eye-Level Rule

Hang art so its centre is about **145 to 152 cm (57 to 60 inches) from the floor**. Above furniture, leave 20 to 30 cm between the furniture and the bottom of the canvas. Our guide on [how to hang canvas wall art](/blog/how-to-hang-large-canvas-prints) explains the measuring step by step.

## Rule 3: On an Empty Wall, Fill About Half

On a wall with no furniture in front, aim for art that covers roughly half to two-thirds of the open wall width.

## Size by Room

### Living Room

- Above a 3-seater sofa: one 30x48 canvas, or two 24x36 canvases.
- Above an L-shaped sofa: one 36x60 canvas on the longer wall.
- Feature wall with no furniture: one 36x60 canvas.

See our [living room canvas wall art guide](/blog/ultimate-guide-wall-canvas-living-room) for style ideas.

### Bedroom

- Above a queen bed: one 24x36 canvas landscape, or two 18x24 canvases.
- Above a king bed: one 30x48 canvas, or two 24x36 canvases.

More ideas in [canvas painting for the bedroom](/blog/styling-bedroom-modern-canvas-art).

### Dining Room

The art should be about two-thirds the length of the table. A 30x48 canvas suits a six-seater table.

### Home Office

Behind the desk, an 18x24 or 24x36 canvas frames you well on video calls. See our [office wall art ideas](/blog/transforming-home-offices-wall-canvas).

### Hallways and Staircases

Use portrait-oriented 12x18 or 18x24 canvases, or a series of the same size hung at a consistent height.

## One Large Canvas or Several?

- **One large canvas** is calmer, more luxurious and easier to hang. Best for minimal and modern interiors.
- **Two or three canvases** are better for very wide walls, or when a single huge canvas will not fit through the door or lift.

## Try Before You Buy

Cut newspaper or kraft paper to the size you are considering and tape it to the wall. Live with it for a day. It is the easiest way to see whether a size is right.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What size canvas goes above a sofa?

About two-thirds the width of the sofa. For a standard 3-seater, a 30x48 inch canvas, or two 24x36 inch canvases.

### What size canvas goes above a queen bed?

A 24x36 inch canvas hung landscape, or two 18x24 inch canvases side by side.

### What is the most popular canvas size?

24x36 inches is the most versatile size. It suits beds, TV units and medium sofas.

### What canvas sizes does GPSFDK offer?

From 12x18 up to 36x60 inches, and square designs from 12x12 to 48x48 inches, stretched or rolled.

## Conclusion

Getting the size right is the simplest way to make wall art look professional. Use the two-thirds rule above furniture, hang the centre at eye level, and test with a paper template before you order.
