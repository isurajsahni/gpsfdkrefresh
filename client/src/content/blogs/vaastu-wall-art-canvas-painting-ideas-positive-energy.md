# Vastu Paintings for Home: 7 Canvas Art Ideas for Positive Energy

For many Indian families, choosing wall art is not only about looks. Vastu Shastra (also spelt Vaastu), the traditional Indian science of space and direction, suggests that the images on our walls influence the energy of a home. The right **Vastu painting** in the right place is believed to support harmony, health and prosperity.

Here are seven popular Vastu canvas art ideas, where traditional guidance suggests placing them, and what to avoid. These are traditional beliefs rather than rules, so treat them as a guide and choose art you also love.

## 1. Seven Running Horses: Success and Momentum

Seven white horses running together are one of the most popular Vastu paintings in India, symbolising speed, strength and progress.

- **Where:** traditionally on the south wall of the living room or office, or the east wall.
- **Tip:** choose horses running into the room, not out of it, and avoid images where they look aggressive.

## 2. Landscapes and Mountains: Stability

Mountains represent support and stability.

- **Where:** traditionally on the south or south-west wall, often behind a work desk to symbolise backing.
- **Tip:** calm, green or blue landscapes are preferred over stormy or barren scenes.

## 3. Flowing Water: Abundance

Gentle waterfalls, rivers and seascapes are linked to the flow of wealth and opportunity.

- **Where:** traditionally on the north wall, associated with prosperity.
- **Tip:** avoid water art in the bedroom, and avoid rough, stormy seas anywhere.

## 4. Floral Art: Love and Positivity

Fresh, blooming **floral art** is considered auspicious and uplifting, and is one of the easiest Vastu-friendly choices for modern homes.

- **Where:** living rooms, dining areas and bedrooms. Pairs of flowers are often recommended for couples' bedrooms in the south-west.
- **Tip:** choose flowers in full bloom with bright, positive colours. Avoid wilted or dried flowers.

## 5. Divine and Spiritual Art: Blessings

Images of Lord Ganesha, Lakshmi, Krishna, Buddha or a family deity are widely used to invite blessings.

- **Where:** traditionally the north-east, the most sacred direction, or the pooja room. Lord Ganesha is often placed facing the main entrance.
- **Tip:** avoid placing deity images in the bedroom or bathroom, or on a wall shared with a bathroom.

## 6. Green Forests and Trees: Growth

Lush forests and trees represent growth, health and new beginnings.

- **Where:** traditionally the east wall, associated with health and growth, and suitable for living rooms and study areas.
- **Tip:** green, sunlit forests are preferred over dark or bare trees.

## 7. Family Photos and Portraits: Togetherness

Happy family portraits are believed to strengthen bonds.

- **Where:** traditionally the south-west wall of the living room.
- **Tip:** choose smiling, happy photos. A large **custom photo canvas** has more presence than a small personal photo frame. See our [custom photo canvas guide](/blog/photo-to-canvas-memories-gallery-art).

## Abstract Art and Vastu

Abstract art is not traditional, but many Vastu guides suggest it is fine when the colours are positive. Calm blues, greens, warm earth tones and gold are generally preferred, while harsh black-and-red combinations are best kept to offices. Layered abstracts in [Tethered Horizons](/wall-canvas/tethered-horizons) suit this approach.

## What to Avoid in Vastu Wall Art

- War, violence, crying faces or sad, lonely figures
- Wild animals attacking or hunting
- Sinking ships, storms or natural disasters
- Thorny plants, cactus and dried flowers
- Images of ruins or broken objects

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which painting is good for the home as per Vastu?

Popular Vastu paintings include seven running horses, flowing water, mountains, blooming flowers, lotus, green forests, deities and happy family portraits.

### Which direction should a Vastu painting face?

It depends on the subject. Traditional guidance places water in the north, mountains in the south or south-west, forests in the east, and deities in the north-east.

### Is floral art good as per Vastu?

Yes. Fresh, blooming flowers are considered auspicious. Avoid wilted or dried flowers and thorny plants.

### Can I hang abstract art as per Vastu?

Yes, if the colours are calm and positive. Soft blues, greens, earth tones and gold are generally preferred.

## Conclusion

Vastu wall art blends tradition with modern design. Choose positive, uplifting subjects, place them in the suggested direction where you can, and above all pick canvas art your family will enjoy every day.
