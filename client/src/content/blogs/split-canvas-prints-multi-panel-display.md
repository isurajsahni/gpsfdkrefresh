# Multi-Panel Canvas Wall Art: How to Plan a Split Display

One canvas is a statement. Two or three canvases hung together as a set, a pair, triptych or larger arrangement, can fill a wide wall in a way a single piece cannot. This guide explains how to plan multi-panel canvas wall art: which images work, how to size and space the panels, and how to hang them perfectly level.

## What Is Multi-Panel Canvas Art?

Multi-panel (or split) canvas art is a set of canvases designed to be seen together. There are two main types:

- **Split canvas:** one image divided across several panels, so the picture continues from one canvas to the next.
- **Coordinated set:** separate artworks from the same collection, chosen to share colours and style, hung side by side.

Common layouts:

- **Diptych:** two panels
- **Triptych:** three panels, the most popular
- **Five panels:** for very wide walls, often in staggered heights

## Why Choose Multiple Canvases?

### Fill Wide Walls

Above a long sofa, a dining table or a bed with side tables, a set of canvases covers the width more gracefully than one oversized piece.

### Easier to Carry and Hang

Several medium canvases are easier to move through Indian apartment doors, lifts and staircases than one enormous canvas.

### Flexible Layouts

You can space panels further apart on wide walls, closer on narrow ones, or rearrange them if you move house.

### A Modern Look

The gaps between panels add rhythm and make the art feel architectural.

## Best Images for Multi-Panel Displays

### Abstract Art

Abstracts are the easiest choice because there is no face or key object to be cut by a gap. Layered pieces from [Tethered Horizons](/wall-canvas/tethered-horizons) and geometric work from [Ink & Interval](/wall-canvas/ink--interval) work well as coordinated sets.

### Landscapes and Skies

Wide horizons, mountains, seascapes and night skies naturally stretch across panels. See [The Celestial Frontier](/wall-canvas/the-celestial-frontier) for cosmic scenes.

### Floral Sets

Two or three florals from the same collection, such as [The Botanical Muse](/wall-canvas/the-botanical-muse), make a soft, elegant set for bedrooms and dining rooms.

### Images to Avoid

- Close-up faces, where a gap would cut through eyes or mouths
- Images with text across the middle
- Low-resolution photos, since each panel enlarges part of the image

## Sizing and Spacing

### The Overall Width

Use the two-thirds rule: the whole group, including gaps, should be about two-thirds the width of the furniture below it.

| Furniture width | Suggested set |
|---|---|
| 150 cm (60 in) TV unit or bed | Two 18x24 inch canvases |
| 180 cm (72 in) 3-seater sofa | Three 18x24 inch canvases, or two 24x36 inch |
| 220 cm (87 in) or wider | Three 24x36 inch canvases |

### The Gaps

Keep 5 to 8 cm between panels. Smaller gaps read as one image; larger gaps make each panel stand alone. Whatever you choose, keep every gap identical.

More sizing help is in our [canvas size guide](/blog/wall-canvas-size-guide-living-room-layouts).

## How to Hang a Multi-Panel Set

### Tools

Measuring tape, spirit level, pencil, painter's tape, drill and wall plugs, and a helper.

### The Process

1. Find the centre of the wall or furniture and mark it.
2. Hang the **middle panel first**, centred on that mark.
3. Measure the gap and hang the next panel on each side, checking the level across all panels.
4. Check that the top edges line up (or that staggered panels are symmetrical).

### Pro Tip: The Paper Template Method

Cut paper to the size of each canvas, tape the templates to the wall with the right gaps, and mark the hook position on each one. Drill through the marks, remove the paper, and hang.

## Common Mistakes

- Uneven gaps between panels
- Hanging the set too high above the furniture
- Choosing a set that is too narrow for the wall
- Mixing collections with clashing colours

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How far apart should split canvas panels be?

Keep 5 to 8 cm between panels, and make every gap exactly the same.

### Is a single large canvas or a split set better?

A single canvas is calmer and simpler to hang. A set is better for very wide walls, tight staircases and lifts, or when you want a more dynamic look.

### What art works best across several panels?

Abstract art, landscapes and night skies work best because nothing important is cut by the gaps.

## Conclusion

Multi-panel canvas wall art is the best way to fill a wide wall with style. Choose images without key details in the gaps, size the whole set to two-thirds of the furniture, keep the gaps even, and hang from the middle out.
